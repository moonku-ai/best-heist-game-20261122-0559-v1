The Midnight Gallery: a one-room top-down heist game. Sneak past two patrolling guards with vision cones and two sweeping wall cameras, steal the Star Diamond and the gold, and slip out the EXIT before the alarm hits 100% or dawn breaks. One room, all of it visible in a single frame: the thief, the guards and their cones, the cameras and theirs, the loot and the alarm meter. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best heist game" bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and play with WASD or arrow keys — or click where the thief should go. Space starts, P pauses.
